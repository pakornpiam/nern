TopoPuzzle

Turn any place on Earth into an interlocking, 3D-printable topographic puzzle. Pick a spot, generate the terrain, export STL pieces.

1 · Location

Enter a region or province above and press this button, or leave the search empty and click the region on the map. The terrain will follow its administrative boundary.
Drag the green box to move the puzzle area, or drag a corner to resize it. Click anywhere else on the map to recentre.
Import a GPX route (e.g. from gpx.studio) — the puzzle area auto-fits the track.
Draw: click the map to add points, type coordinates above, or 📍 for your GPS location; double-click to finish. Edit: drag a point to move it, click/drag a small midpoint to insert, right-click a point to delete.
The 3.2 mm route reaches the print bottom. Its channel is 3.6 mm wide, leaving 0.2 mm clearance on each side for assembly.

2 · Puzzle

The 1 × 1 default is a single terrain model with no puzzle cuts.

Kept only in this tab. Manage usage and charges with MapTiler. Detail varies by location: 30 m globally, 5 m in selected areas.

Get a MapTiler key
Detail sets the grid on the longer side; the shorter side scales to match so terrain cells stay square. Tile spacing is separate from source accuracy. Very high settings use more time and memory.

Flat-roof buildings appear in the preview and exports. Missing heights use floor counts or a 9 m estimate. Select a neighborhood for visible city detail.
